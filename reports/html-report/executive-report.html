        Analysis

        What the Results Suggest

        August 20265

        Membership is still strong.

        Why: Active membership is 525,382, up 25.0% versus July 2025 and up 81,139 year to date.

        Recommendation: Keep leading with the strength of the membership base, but do not let the top-line number hide softness underneath.

        Acquisition is softer than last year.

        Why: New GC golfers are down 2.3% YTD versus Jan-July 2025, and July new golfers were down 14.1% versus July 2025.

        Recommendation: Treat acquisition as the primary growth problem to solve for the next planning cycle.

        Retention is doing a lot of the work.

        Why: July on-time renewal is 83.4%, well above 66.0% last July. July 12-month rolling retention is 79.0%, also above last year.

        Recommendation: Protect the retention gains, but recognize that retention is offsetting acquisition softness rather than replacing the need for new demand.

        Recovery/win-back looks like a practical opportunity.

        Why: Recoveries are 39,664 YTD, up 30.8% versus Jan-July 2025. Recovered members are coming from creation years as far back as 2022.

        Recommendation: Continue the win-back test and define what performance would justify automating it nationally.

        Paid Media and source attribution need clearer interpretation.

        Why: Paid Media contributes 25,904 YTD conversions, but source mix still has a large Organic / Untagged bucket.

        Recommendation: Evaluate Paid Media with both conversion output and demand-creation context, not just CPA or total conversions.

        The bigger question is how we create more demand, not just capture people already close to signing up.

        Why: Acquisition is down despite continued conversion activity. The issue may also be the story, audience, and reason golfers feel motivated to get a Handicap Index.

        Recommendation: Use the next growth meeting to separate short-term conversion optimization from the longer-term work of creating broader demand for GC membership.
